00 · Executive summary16 weeks

ePAK by Pak2Go, a responsive platform to quote, buy, and manage shipping labels for businesses and individuals who sell and distribute products in Mexico.

16wk

From research to tested prototype

400

Online surveys to people who sell online

35%

Of visitors registered before the redesign

4

Competitors analyzed in detail

Problem

The old platform worked but wasn't friendly: hard shipment creation, few payment options, and unclear tracking.

Approach

Design thinking in 16 weeks: benchmark, interviews, 400 surveys, IA, wireframes, and usability studies.

Outcome

A refreshed platform with guided workflows, new sections, more payment options, and real-time tracking.
