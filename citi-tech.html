00 · Executive summary~6 months

Six new features for the Citibanamex mobile app, designed and released one by one with the New York team: QR payments, CoDi refunds, direct debits, service payments, Paybal, and recharges.

6

Features shipped in six months

357

Usability studies across modules

28k+

Surveys across the six modules

78%

Satisfaction after progressive release

Problem

The app lacked key features users demanded, risking satisfaction and competitiveness.

Approach

A six-month roadmap of features prioritized by impact, each designed, prototyped, and tested in turn.

Outcome

New payment features released gradually, with success rates between 71% and 94% after testing.
