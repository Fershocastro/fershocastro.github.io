NTT PASS by NTT Data, a ticket app for safer events during COVID-19: attendees verify their identity and a test result before they can access national and international events.

Problem

With the pandemic, NTT Data needed an app to manage attendance at entertainment events.

Approach

Online research, personas, a new IA, wireframes, and usability testing over twelve weeks.

Outcome

An app where people buy tickets, verify their ID, upload a test, and get a pass to enter.

Step 01 / 05Weeks 1–3
01

Empathize

GoalUnderstand users and the COVID-19 context before designing anything.

Mixed research
  • Surveys on current experience, concerns, and the features that make people feel safe.
  • Documentary research from medical sources, government reports, and the entertainment industry.
  • Context: PCR as the gold standard, antigen tests for speed, and a 40% jump in online ticket sales.

Step 03 / 05Weeks 6–7
03

Ideate

GoalFour themes from brainstorming, and an architecture that leads to a pass.

THEME 1

Interactive onboarding

Tooltips, short tutorials, gamification, and a progress tracker.

THEME 2

Easy event addition

Step by step, smart suggestions, and reminders.

Efficient testing

A guide, easy upload, chat support, and a status summary.

Robust measures, two-factor auth, and awareness.
