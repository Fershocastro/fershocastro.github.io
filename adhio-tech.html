00 · Executive summary1-day sprint

A conversion-focused landing page, designed from scratch in a single day, for ADHIO: an AI Chrome extension that writes high-impact LinkedIn posts and profiles.

1day

From kickoff to hi-fi prototype

5

Sprint steps, compressed into one day

4

Competitors benchmarked across 7 variables

30

Users in the NPS survey

Problem

The company needed a landing page from scratch: user-friendly and conversion-focused, on a very tight timeline.

Approach

A one-day design sprint with Scrum-based operations: map, sketch, decide, prototype, and test.

Outcome

A clear narrative page that explains the tool's value, builds trust, and drives Chrome installs.

Objectives
  • Communicate the tool's value clearly.
  • Convert visitors into leads.
  • Ensure a seamless, intuitive experience.
Key challengePlanning, design, and testing had to fit into one day without losing quality.

  • Must-have elements: a clear CTA, trust signals, and a compelling hero.

Deciding

Step 04 / 05Late afternoon
04

Prototyping

GoalBuild basic versions of the page with interactive components and mapped micro-interactions.

Page sections
  • Suggestive hero: a short explainer video and client logos for credibility.
  • Benefits: time saved, better engagement, and data-driven insights.
  • Metrics & testimonials: proof of effectiveness and real user stories.
  • Real examples & FAQs: before/after analytics, install, and safety.
Key decisionPrototype only key elements, buttons, forms, and CTAs, to keep the sprint efficient while testing realistic interactions.
