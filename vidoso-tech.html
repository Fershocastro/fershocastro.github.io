New features for Vidoso, an AI video platform: turn long videos into ready-to-publish content, with precise transcription, AI-assisted editing, and copy for every channel.

Problem

Social, marketing, and sales teams needed to create, edit, and share multimedia content in minutes, not hours.

Approach

Iterative work with the client in weekly one-hour sessions, benchmarking, flows, and four rounds of testing.

Outcome

Four key features: AI content generation, a new editor, multichannel flows, and advanced search.

Key tasks
  • Social content flows: visualize and improve how content is generated for social networks.
  • New video editor: built from up to 3 tested proposals presented to the client.
  • Multichannel creation: an AI-enriched flow tailored to each channel.
  • Search experience: advanced filtering and new features.

Step 01 / 05Weeks 1–2
01

Mapping

GoalBuild a value proposition from the client's vision and user insights, and find where AI plays a real role.

What we did
  • Online refinement sessions with the client (one hour each) on requirements, progress, and decisions.
  • Visual and functional benchmark of 6 competitors across 8 variables.
  • Defined features for implementation, prioritizing AI, agile editing, and responsive versions.
  • Agreed on the target with the client: communication and marketing pros and entrepreneurs aged 22–45.
Key insightNo competitor combined fast creation, strong AI generation, and social integration. That intersection became Vidoso's focus.

Step 04 / 05Weeks 5–7
04

Prototyping & testing

GoalTest, iterate, and refine features with real users before development.

What we did
  • Feedback from 20+ users across four usability studies.
  • Grouped comments into common themes to find key areas.
  • Measured how often each problem appeared and explored its nature.
  • Prioritized improvements by severity and impact on the experience.
