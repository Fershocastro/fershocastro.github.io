00 · Executive summary3-day sprint

Tori, a financial virtual assistant inside the Stori app, a Mexican credit card for people building their credit history, to help them pay on time and learn about money.

3days

From research to hi-fi prototype

40

Users surveyed and interviewed online

70%

Had experienced payment delays

85%

Praised the clarity of Tori's explanations

Problem

Users exceeded their limits or paid late, and most lacked financial education.

Approach

A three-day design-thinking sprint: survey, personas, journey, flows, and a hi-fi prototype.

Outcome

A basic or advanced assistant with reminders, a financial profile, challenges, and a ChatGPT-powered chat.

MVP definition
  • Customized plans: a financial profile from card history and estimated expenses.
  • Smart analysis: financial behavior and due-date reminders.
  • Educational content: key concepts and smart challenges.
  • Basic and premium features for different needs.
Success metricsROIUser retention

Step 01 / 05Day 1
01

Empathize

GoalProve the need for an assistant with real numbers from users.

How we researched
  • Online survey to 40 mobile-banking users with credit cards, chosen at random.
  • Google Forms, shared mainly on WhatsApp and Facebook to move fast.
  • Interviews with close contacts, plus documentary research.
  • Push notifications research: 76% accept them if relevant and well-timed.

Step 04 / 05Days 2–3
04

Prototype

GoalFrom low-fi wireframes to a hi-fi prototype, within Stori's brand.

What we did
  • Low-fi wireframes for 10 key screens.
  • Style guide based on Stori's brand, adjusted for consistency and accessibility.
  • Financial profile setup with three question types: ranked answers, checklists, and multiple selection.
  • Hi-fi prototype with onboarding, assistant choice, chat, and reminders.
