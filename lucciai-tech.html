The MVP of Lucci, an enterprise AI CRM that automates sales 24/7: it manages multi-platform conversations, trains on each store's products, and turns chats into orders.

Problem

Retail and wholesale businesses needed AI support to sell, but CRM tools felt complex and hard to integrate.

Approach

A five-step sprint: mapping, sketching, deciding, prototyping, and testing with business owners.

Outcome

A CRM where an AI agent sells 24/7 on WhatsApp, learns the catalog, and reports on performance.

Design principles
  • Scalability: grow with users and data.
  • Security: protect sensitive data and platform integrity.
  • Interoperability: integrate with the apps businesses already use.
  • Analytics: data for informed, continuous improvement.
  • Support: a maintenance plan that keeps it relevant.

Step 01 / 05Sprint · Days 1–3
01

Mapping

GoalUnderstand needs and challenges to define the core of an AI-powered CRM MVP.

What we did
  • Semi-structured interviews (30–45 min) with sales, marketing, IT, and service roles across business sizes and channels.
  • Explored current sales and CRM methods, expectations, technical constraints, and adoption barriers.
  • Benchmarked Intercom FIN, Chatspot, Copy AI, Zapier, and Tidio across 11 variables.
  • Defined three personas and the target: people 25+ looking to sell more with AI.
Key insightCompetitors offer strong AI replies, but none make learning easy. That gap became the opportunity: an AI CRM that's simple from day one.

Step 04 / 05Sprint · Days 8–10
04

Prototyping

GoalTurn decisions into a hi-fi prototype, aligned with dev and accessibility criteria.

Key flows
  • Login & sign-up: multi-channel and multilingual, up to 8 languages.
  • Chat management: filtering, new channels, prioritization, real-time sentiment, and AI templates.
  • Inventory & orders: upload products, manage orders, and optimize sales flows.
  • Train Lucci: guided setup so the AI learns products, markets, and data policies.
Key decisionAn enterprise version with ethical training options, plus a trial; and a landing page to grow the demo's user base for broader feedback.
